Lipid-lowering therapy response calculator

Estimates the expected LDL-C at 3 months after a treatment change by back-calculating the untreated baseline LDL from the current regimen and applying the multiplicative reduction of the new regimen.

Method: LDLbaseline = LDLcurrent / (1 − rcurrent) · LDLpredicted = LDLbaseline × (1 − rnew) · For combinations: rtotal = 1 − Π(1 − ri) · Reduction values from Law meta-analysis (BMJ 2003), ESC 2021 and PCSK9/inclisiran trials.

Limitations: Percentages are population means — individual response varies ±15–20% due to genetic factors (SLCO1B1, APOE variants), adherence, diet, and absorption. The prediction assumes full adherence. In familial hypercholesterolaemia, statin response may be lower. Does not account for Lp(a), TG or HDL.
Clinical use: Decision-support tool. Does not replace follow-up bloodwork at 8–12 weeks after any treatment change.
